How this page works
Every supplier invoice Mindy saves to her Invoices folder is read automatically (late morning and mid-afternoon on weekdays) and matched to the ServiceTitan job it was bought for. The invoice's amount due equals the job's PO/Bill cost in ServiceTitan to the cent, so most invoices match exactly; when the amount can't decide it, the customer named on the invoice or the tech who ordered it is used. Each invoice is filed under the job's department; invoices with no job yet are under General.

What to check: do the parts on the left belong on the job on the right? Were they billed to the customer (or covered by the task that was sold)? Does the write-up mention the work they'd be used for? When you’re done, slide it to Reviewed. If there’s a problem, write it in the note box and click Save; it shows as Problem noted in the list (Show → Has a problem note).

Pick the job = the amount fits more than one job; click the right one. Not matched yet = no job matches the amount yet. It's re-checked every day for 3 weeks (an install that isn't finished, or a bill that hasn't posted). After that it shows as No job found; that's usually truck stock. Stock / shop orders have no job. Scanned PDF = this supplier sends a picture of the invoice that can't be read automatically. Open the PDF.

Costs come from the daily PO Cost Refresh report (all departments, last 7 days). Invoices you mark here are shared: everyone sees the same marks.
